Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Data collection

EspToolbox does not collect, share or transmit any personal data. The app does not require creating any account.

Data on your device

All configurations and information related to your ESP boards are stored exclusively on your Android device, locally.

Permissions

The app uses USB access only to communicate with the connected ESP boards. No other permission is used for tracking purposes.

Third-party services

EspToolbox does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services.
