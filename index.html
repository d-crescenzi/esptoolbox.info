About the app

EspToolbox lets you easily configure and manage ESP32 and ESP8266 boards directly from your Android smartphone over a USB connection.

Key features

  • Wi-Fi configuration

    Set up the board's Wi-Fi network.

  • Erase flash

    Wipe the flash memory of the connected board.

  • Firmware flashing

    Flash compatible firmware onto the board.

  • Serial diagnostics

    Diagnostics and serial communication.

EspToolbox is the ideal tool for makers, developers and technicians working with ESP-based devices.
